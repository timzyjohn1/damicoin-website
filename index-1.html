Current technical status

Our Solidity token prototype compiled and deployed in Remix VM, a local simulated blockchain. Basic token name, symbol, supply, and wallet balance reads were verified.

It has not yet been deployed to public testnet or mainnet and has not received an independent security audit.

Transparency

Frequently asked questions.

Can I buy DAMI right now?

No. DAMICOIN is not publicly launched, and there is no official mainnet token contract or sale.

Is DAMICOIN on BNB Smart Chain?

Not yet. The prototype was deployed in Remix VM, which is a simulation. Public testnet and mainnet deployments are future milestones.

Does DAMI guarantee profit or price growth?

No. The project makes no promises about price, returns, liquidity, exchange listings, or future success.

What is the planned token supply?

The current prototype uses a supply of 1 billion tokens with 18 decimals. The final public launch configuration and distribution plan remain subject to review.

How can I follow the project?

Official channels are not yet published. We will add links only after accounts are established and controlled by the project. Be cautious of impersonators, unsolicited offers, and fake giveaways.
